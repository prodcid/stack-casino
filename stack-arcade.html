HOW THE CLAW WORKS
  1. Point at a box. A tag over it and the green screen show what it's worth and your chance to win it, before you pay anything.
  2. Press PLAY (or space). You have 15 seconds to line up. Get the claw over the middle of the box: LINE-UP 100% means you get the full chance.
  3. Drop it: click the glass or press space. Win and the box drops down the chute for you to unbox.

Your chance depends on the box's finish (the sticker on the box). The rarer the finish, the bigger the prize and the smaller the chance. Every box pays back 97% on average:
